alpha kappa psi community relations

background: alpha kappa psi is a co-ed professional fraternity, and directorships make up the organization. community relations is a directorship that runs events facing the rest of the study body before official recruitment. instagram

role: as project manager, i coordinated 3 team members to organize 6 campus-wide events based on surveying student focus groups to raise brand awareness.

the reality: students know business fraternities are rigorous, but don’t know why.

the tension: students know they would have to dedicate time, but alpha kappa psi doesn’t demonstrate why it’s a worthy investment.

the strategic problem: how can alpha kappa psi communicate the value it provides to its members to an outsider?

my insight

students’ time is precious. it’s not required to join a student organization, so a reason must be provided.

my response: built 6 events demonstrating 4 core values of the organization: professional development volunteering student leadership campus presence

my impact: ~300 attendance across all events. 78% conversion from pre-events to official recruitment events.

skills: event planning design project management surveys
