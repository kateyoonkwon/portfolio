minturn whisky

background: minturn whisky is a local colarado distillery who wanted a rock-solid brand strategy. instagram website

role: as a brand marketer, i identified 3 business opportunities, building the brand in a united direction

now, minturn whisky’s social media strategy tells their story, defining their brand with their experience-driven, high-quality, scottish flair.

my response: 5c, 4p, competitor, customer journeys analyses to uncover the optimal value proposition.

my impact: clear 90 day roadmaps for 3 business opportunities: building relationships through instagram; reputation through competitions; experiences through expanded distribution channels.

minturn whisky has since participated in the North American Bourbon and Whiskey competition.

skills: brand strategy marketing analytics social media
